03 / THEOREM OF THE DAY

The Pythagorean Theorem

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

a² + b² = c²

For example, if a = 3 and b = 4, then c² = 9 + 16 = 25, meaning c = 5. This relationship connects algebra with geometry and appears in countless applications.
